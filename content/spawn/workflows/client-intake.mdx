## Goal

Turn a vague request into a project hub with clear deliverables.

## Steps

1. Capture contacts, timeline, and budget notes
2. Gather references and technical constraints
3. Create a project hub under [Active Projects](/spawn/projects/active)
4. Point the hub at the SOPs and workflows you expect to use
